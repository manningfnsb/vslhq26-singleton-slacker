REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL: Cloud-Based Employee Management System
Organization: Fairbanks North Star Borough
RFP Number: FNSB-2025-IT-042
Issue Date: January 15, 2025
Due Date: March 1, 2025

1. INTRODUCTION
The Fairbanks North Star Borough seeks proposals for a cloud-based Employee Management System (EMS).
The system must support approximately 1,200 employees across 15 departments.

2. TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS
REQ-001: The system shall provide single sign-on (SSO) integration with Microsoft Entra ID.
REQ-002: The system shall support role-based access control (RBAC) with a minimum of 5 configurable roles.
REQ-003: All data must be encrypted at rest using AES-256 and in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher.
REQ-004: The system shall maintain 99.9% uptime measured monthly.
REQ-005: The system must provide a RESTful API for integration with existing payroll systems.
REQ-006: The vendor shall provide disaster recovery with RPO of 4 hours and RTO of 8 hours.

3. FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS
REQ-007: The system shall include employee onboarding workflows with configurable approval chains.
REQ-008: The system must support time and attendance tracking with geofencing capabilities.
REQ-009: The system shall generate automated compliance reports for federal and state labor regulations.
REQ-010: The system must provide a self-service portal for employees to update personal information, view pay stubs, and request leave.

4. VENDOR REQUIREMENTS
REQ-011: The vendor must provide 24/7 technical support with a guaranteed response time of 2 hours for critical issues.
REQ-012: The vendor must provide on-site training for up to 50 system administrators.
REQ-013: The vendor shall provide a data migration plan for transitioning from the legacy PeopleSoft system.
REQ-014: The vendor must comply with FedRAMP Moderate or equivalent security certification.
REQ-015: The contract term shall be 3 years with two optional 1-year extensions.
