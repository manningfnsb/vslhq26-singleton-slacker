PROPOSAL: Cloud-Based Employee Management System
Submitted by: NorthTech Solutions, LLC
Date: February 20, 2025
In response to: RFP FNSB-2025-IT-042

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
NorthTech Solutions is pleased to submit this proposal for the Employee Management System. Our platform, WorkforceOne, has served over 200 government clients nationwide.

2. TECHNICAL APPROACH
WorkforceOne supports SSO integration with Microsoft Entra ID, Okta, and other SAML 2.0 providers. Our RBAC module includes 8 pre-configured roles with full customization capabilities.

All data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3. Data at rest is encrypted using AES-256 across all storage layers including database, file storage, and backups.

Our platform runs on Microsoft Azure with geo-redundant deployments. We guarantee 99.95% uptime backed by SLA credits. Our API layer provides RESTful endpoints documented via OpenAPI 3.0 specification for seamless integration with payroll and HRIS systems.

3. DISASTER RECOVERY
We provide automated daily backups with point-in-time recovery. Our disaster recovery plan targets an RPO of 1 hour and RTO of 4 hours, exceeding industry standards.

4. FUNCTIONAL CAPABILITIES
WorkforceOne includes a comprehensive onboarding module with configurable multi-step approval workflows. Hiring managers can customize onboarding checklists per department.

Our time and attendance module supports mobile clock-in with GPS verification. Note: Geofencing is on our product roadmap for Q3 2025 and is not currently available.

The self-service employee portal allows staff to view pay stubs, update contact information, and submit leave requests with manager approval workflows.

5. SUPPORT AND TRAINING
NorthTech provides business-hours support (Monday-Friday, 8AM-6PM AKST) with 4-hour response time for critical issues. Premium 24/7 support is available as an add-on package.

We offer virtual training sessions for administrators. On-site training can be arranged at additional cost for groups up to 25 participants per session.

6. COMPLIANCE AND SECURITY
WorkforceOne is SOC 2 Type II certified and complies with HIPAA requirements. We are currently pursuing FedRAMP authorization and expect to achieve Moderate certification by Q4 2025.

7. CONTRACT TERMS
We propose a 3-year base contract with options for annual renewal thereafter. Pricing is detailed in Attachment B.
